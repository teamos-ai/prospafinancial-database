# Prospa Financial education sessions

> **Scheduling note:** The in-person workshop is intended for next week. Date and time are not yet confirmed.

## 1. From High Income to Financial Independence

### Delivery

- **Format:** Face-to-face workshop
- **Venue:** Prospa Financial office — Level 1, 36 Mills Street, Albert Park VIC 3206
- **Duration:** 60–90 minutes
- **Audience:** Senior leaders and managers in their peak earning years
- **Session description:** A practical, thought-provoking session for senior leaders who want to turn strong earnings into lasting wealth, greater financial flexibility and the option to make work optional.

### Session purpose

High income creates opportunity, but income alone does not create financial independence. This session explores how senior professionals can use their peak earning years more deliberately to build, protect and ultimately transfer wealth.

The focus is education and general information, using practical examples to highlight financial questions worth exploring further.

### Agenda

| # | Topic | Time | Focus |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | The Financial Freedom Gap | 5 min | Why a high income does not automatically create wealth, and how peak earning years can create long-term choice. |
| 2 | Superannuation: Make It Work Harder | 10 min | Contribution strategies, investment settings and tax-effective opportunities that may build retirement capital efficiently. |
| 3 | Building Wealth Outside Super | 10 min | Directing surplus cash flow into diversified investments while maintaining flexibility before retirement. |
| 4 | Tax-Effective Wealth Structures | 10 min | Where personal ownership, family trusts, investment bonds and other structures may fit depending on goals and circumstances. |
| 5 | Making Work Optional | 10 min | Working backwards from a desired lifestyle and timeframe to consider required capital and income. |
| 6 | Protecting What You Have Built | 10 min | Income protection, life, TPD, trauma, liquidity and debt management as part of a resilient financial plan. |
| 7 | Passing on Wealth to the Next Generation | 10 min | Estate planning, super beneficiary nominations and intergenerational wealth considerations. |
| 8 | Your Executive Financial Health Check | 5 min | Key questions to test whether tax, investments, super, protection and estate planning are working together. |
| 9 | Live Q&A | 10–15 min | Open forum for general questions. Personal circumstances can be discussed separately in a confidential setting. |

### Optional follow-up

Attendees may book a complimentary, confidential one-on-one initial consultation with a Prospa Financial adviser. There is no obligation to proceed.

### Key takeaways

- Clarity on financial direction
- Smarter use of peak earning years
- Tax-aware wealth-building concepts
- A pathway to work becoming optional
- Confidence about next steps

## 2. Your Super. Your Future. Make It Count.

### Delivery

- **Format:** Virtual webinar — interactive presentation with Q&A
- **Expected attendance:** Approximately 50–70 people
- **Duration:** 60–75 minutes
- **Audience:** Employees at all career and life stages
- **Session description:** A practical, easy-to-understand session to help employees build confidence, make informed decisions and get more from their superannuation.

### Session purpose

For many Australians, superannuation will become one of their largest financial assets outside the family home, yet it is often one of the least understood.

This session is designed to improve employees’ financial literacy and confidence by helping them understand how super works, the decisions that can affect their retirement outcomes, and common mistakes to avoid. The session focuses on education and general information, not individual financial advice.

### Agenda

| # | Topic | Time | Focus |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Superannuation: Why It Deserves Your Attention | 5 min | Why super matters, the power of compounding and how it can support financial freedom. |
| 2 | Understanding Your Super Statement | 10 min | Contributions, investment returns, fees, insurance and beneficiary nominations. |
| 3 | Where Is Your Super Actually Invested? | 10 min | Investment options, risk and return, and why investment timeframe matters. |
| 4 | The Superannuation “Tricks and Traps” | 10 min | Common mistakes that can affect super, including multiple accounts, insurance issues, fees and contribution limits. |
| 5 | How to Build Your Super More Effectively | 10 min | Contribution strategies, government incentives and simple examples to illustrate long-term impact. |
| 6 | Super and Tax: What You Should Know | 5–10 min | How super is taxed, contribution limits and why it can be a tax-effective investment structure. |
| 7 | Insurance Inside Super: The Often Forgotten Safety Net | 5 min | Life, TPD and income protection, and why it is important to review cover. |
| 8 | Your Super Health Check | 5 min | A simple checklist of key questions to review superannuation. |
| 9 | Live Q&A | 10–15 min | Open forum for general questions. Personal circumstances can be discussed separately in a confidential setting. |

### Optional follow-up

Employees who want to discuss their own circumstances may arrange a confidential one-on-one conversation with a financial adviser after the session. There is no obligation to participate.

### Key takeaways

- Clearer understanding
- Practical strategies
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Confidence to make informed decisions
- Next steps, if required
