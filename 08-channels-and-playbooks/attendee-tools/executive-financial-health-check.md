# Executive Financial Health Check

## Purpose

A private five-minute check-in for high-income professionals. There are no “good” or “bad” scores. The purpose is to identify which parts of a financial strategy are clear and which may deserve a closer look.

## Response key

For each statement, select one:

- **Green:** I know this is covered.
- **Amber:** I think so, but I am not certain.
- **Red:** This needs attention.

## 1. Cash Flow & Wealth Creation

- [ ] After tax, lifestyle and debt commitments, do I know how much surplus cash flow I create each year?
- [ ] Is that surplus automatically directed toward wealth creation, or does it tend to disappear into lifestyle?
- [ ] Do I have a deliberate strategy for bonuses, incentives or other irregular income?

## 2. Superannuation

- [ ] Do I know whether my current contribution strategy is making effective use of the opportunities available to me?
- [ ] Do I understand what my super is invested in, the level of risk I am taking and the fees I am paying?
- [ ] If I want work to become optional before I can access super, am I building enough wealth outside super as well?

## 3. Investments & Tax Structure

- [ ] Are my investments held in structures chosen deliberately for my goals, tax position, access needs and family circumstances?
- [ ] Do I have a documented investment strategy rather than a collection of unrelated assets?
- [ ] Do I understand the tax consequences of where my next investment dollar goes?

## 4. Financial Independence

- [ ] Have I calculated the annual lifestyle income I would want if work became optional?
- [ ] Do I know approximately how much investable capital may be required to support that lifestyle?
- [ ] Do I have a strategy for the years between my preferred work-optional age and when super becomes accessible?

## 5. Protection & Resilience

- [ ] If my income stopped unexpectedly, how long could my current lifestyle and commitments continue?
- [ ] Have I reviewed life, TPD, trauma and income protection in the context of my current income, debt and family responsibilities?
- [ ] Is there enough liquidity available for an unexpected event without having to sell long-term assets at the wrong time?

## 6. Estate & Intergenerational Wealth

- [ ] Are my will, powers of attorney and super beneficiary nominations current and aligned?
- [ ] Do I understand how my major assets would actually pass to my intended beneficiaries?
- [ ] Have I considered how I want wealth to support the next generation, rather than simply what they may inherit?
