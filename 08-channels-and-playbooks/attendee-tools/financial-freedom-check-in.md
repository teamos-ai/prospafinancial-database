# The 2-Minute Financial Freedom Check-In

## Purpose

A short pre-workshop questionnaire for people attending the financial freedom session. It is designed to identify the group’s financial priorities and help tailor the session. Responses are anonymous; there are no right or wrong answers.

## Delivery notes

- **When to send:** Email before the session.
- **Purpose of the email:** Build an understanding of the attendees and their priorities before presenting.
- **Use in the session:** Aggregate anonymous answers can become presentation material.
- **Scoring:** A proposed post-session “Wealth Score” email is an implementation idea, not a scoring model supplied in this source. Do not create or send a score until the scoring rules, disclaimer and approval path are defined.

## Opening copy

> You have worked hard to build your income. The bigger question is what that income is building for you.
>
> Before the workshop, we would love two minutes of your time. Your responses are designed to help us tailor the session around the financial priorities that matter most to the group.
>
> There are no right or wrong answers. Just choose the response that best reflects where you are today.

## Questions

### 1. Fast-forward 10 years. What would make you feel that your income today had been used well?

Select one:

- My home is substantially or completely paid off.
- I have built meaningful investments outside super.
- I am paying less unnecessary tax and investing more efficiently.
- I have enough wealth for work to become optional.
- My family and children are financially secure.
- I have built wealth that can benefit the next generation.
- I am earning well, but I have not really defined what I want the money to achieve.

### 2. You receive an unexpected $50,000 bonus tomorrow. How confident are you that you know the most effective place for that money?

Select one:

- Very confident. It already fits into my strategy.
- I have a reasonable idea, but I would want to compare my options.
- I would probably put it against the mortgage.
- I would probably invest it.
- I would consider super for the potential tax benefits.
- I genuinely would not know which option is most effective for me.

### 3. At what age would you like work to become a choice rather than a financial necessity?

Select one:

- Before 50
- 50 to 55
- 56 to 60
- After 60
- I enjoy working and have not set an age.
- I have never actually calculated whether I could afford to stop.

### 4. Which statement feels closest to your current financial position?

Select one:

- I have a coordinated financial strategy and review it regularly.
- I have accumulated assets, but I am not sure everything is structured optimally.
- I invest and contribute to super, but there is no overarching strategy connecting everything.
- Most of my wealth is concentrated in my home, property or super.
- My income has increased faster than my financial planning has evolved.
- I know I should review things, but finding the time is the problem.

### 5. If we privately put your financial structure under a microscope today, where would you be least confident?

Select one:

- Whether I am using super effectively.
- Whether my investments are structured tax-effectively.
- Whether I am building enough wealth outside super.
- Whether I am on track to retire when I want.
- Whether my insurance still reflects my income and responsibilities.
- Whether my estate plan would actually distribute my wealth as intended.
- Whether all of these pieces are working together.

### 6. Complete this sentence: “If I could leave the workshop with clarity on one financial question, it would be…”

Free text. This question is mandatory and anonymous.

## Suggested aggregate presentation use

Use only group-level, anonymous responses. Examples from the source:

- “42% of the room would like work to become optional before 55.”
- “Only 18% feel very confident about where they would direct an unexpected $50,000.”

## Source notes

- The source references Moneysmart retirement-planning guidance in relation to desired lifestyle, retirement timing and future income sources.
- This is an educational engagement tool. It is not a personal advice questionnaire or a financial-product assessment.
